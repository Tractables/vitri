vitri

Paste a DIMACS CNF, open or drop a file, or pick an example. vitri simplifies the formula and builds a vtree for it; the reduced formula, the tree and the record that recovers the original count come back as the files vitri --out-dir writes. Everything runs in this tab and nothing is uploaded. vitri is also a Rust crate with a command-line tool; its bundle reference describes every file.
